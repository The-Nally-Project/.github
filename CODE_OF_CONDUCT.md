# Code of Conduct

The Nally Project aims to provide a respectful, constructive environment for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Welcome different experience levels and perspectives.
- Give actionable, good-faith feedback.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.
- Keep discussions focused and professional.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, threats, discrimination, intimidation, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and unwanted personal contact are not acceptable.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project stewards and maintainers are responsible for clarifying expectations, reviewing reports fairly, and taking proportionate action. Actions may include a warning, content moderation, temporary restrictions, or removal from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project stewards through the organisation's published contact channel. Include relevant context and links where safe. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

This document applies to project repositories, discussions, events, and other spaces represented as official Nally Project spaces.
